## Photography Style (Prompt DNA)

```
Background: deep ocean #0a1628 or abyss #010611
Lighting: warm amber 2700-3200K from left, cool cyan accent from right
Camera: Canon R5 (product shots) / Hasselblad X2D (hero)
Lens: 85mm f/2.0-2.8 shallow depth of field
Film grain: 5-8% (never 0%, never >10%)
Water: subtle droplets on product surface
Exclusions: NO white backgrounds, NO cold blue lighting, NO cartoon style
```
