   - YFF-049 vs YFF-052 COMPARISON:
     * YFF-049 = FINE grain (2-3mm), 1.5L bag — this product
     * YFF-052 = COARSE grain (2-5mm), 3L bag — Product 048
     The fine grain (YFF-049) is preferred for:
       - Foreground plantings requiring fine substrate
       - Shrimp tanks where fine grain suits shrimp feeding behavior
       - Dense carpeting plants (HC Cuba, Monte Carlo)
     The coarse grain (YFF-052) is preferred for:
       - Larger aquariums needing better water flow through substrate
       - Stem plants with robust root systems
       - Mixed planted + fish community tanks

   - Soil Line Notes:
     YEE markets this under multiple names: "Amazon Soil," "Nature Aqua Soil,"
     "Water Grass Mud" — all refer to the same product family with varying
     marketing names for different markets.

   - pH buffering: Expect pH to settle at 6.2-6.8 in soft tap water conditions.
     In hard water (common in Iraq), buffering capacity may be overwhelmed faster;
     recommend pairing with RO water or water softener products.
