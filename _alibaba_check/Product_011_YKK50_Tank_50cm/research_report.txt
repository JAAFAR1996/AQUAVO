   and style (open top rimless) all align. Model code YKK-50 is the internal factory
   code used by the Alibaba supplier; it does not appear on the English retail pages but
   is consistent with YEE's naming pattern (YKK = plain crystal tank series).

9. NOTES:
   - The YKK-50 code is an internal Alibaba/wholesale code and may not appear on
     consumer retail listings. Retail pages simply describe it by dimensions.
   - The width dimension varies slightly between sources (260mm vs 270mm). This is
     likely a measurement rounding or a minor variant difference — both are the 50cm
     family tank.
   - YEE's small glass tank family also includes 30cm, 40cm, 60cm, and 80cm variants,
     so the 50cm is a confirmed mid-range option in this series.
   - No lid, filter, or light included — pure bare tank as noted on Alibaba.
   - Glass is confirmed low-iron (ultra-clear / super white), not standard float glass.
     This is a significant quality differentiator for the Iraqi market.
   - WACKER (German brand) silicone sealant is used — a premium, food-safe adhesive
     widely regarded as aquarium-safe. This is a strong selling point.
   - Product is manufactured by Weifang Yipin Pet Products Co., Ltd., Shandong, China
     — a major aquarium OEM with 6 factories, 400+ employees, 10,000+ SKUs.
