6. SPECIFICATIONS FOUND:
   - Brand: YEE (乐宠) — Weifang Yipin Pet Products Co., Ltd.
   - Product Code: YYH-189
   - Form: Liquid algaecide
   - Volume: 500ml
   - Product Series Name: "Special for Aquatic Plants" (per SeeTracker Malaysia)
   - Active Ingredient: NOT confirmed from official source.
     Exact chemical compound not publicly disclosed by YEE. Aquarium algaecides of this
     type commonly use glutaraldehyde or similar organic compounds. Do NOT state ingredient
     without confirmation from packaging.
   - Dosage: 10ml per 100L water.
     Perform 25% water change before each treatment.
     Dilute in tank water before adding directly.
     Use once every other day, 2-3 times for full treatment course.
     Increase surface agitation/aeration during and after treatment (dead algae consume
     oxygen as they decompose).
     (Confirmed from multiple retailer descriptions)
   - Treatment Duration: 4-6 days (3 treatment cycles, every other day)
   - Target Algae Types:
     * Black beard/hair algae (BBA)
     * Green hair algae
     * Green spot algae
     * Green water (algae bloom)
     * Brown algae (diatoms)
     * Blue-green algae (cyanobacteria)
     * Moss overgrowth (inhibits moss — see warning below)
   - Safe for Shrimp: YES — confirmed safe for aquatic shrimp per FishyHub Singapore
     listing. Also listed safe for guppies and betta fish.
   - Safe for Plants: YES — specifically marketed as "Special for Aquatic Plants."
     Confirmed: safe for aquatic plants. Does not harm stems, leaves, or roots.
   - Safe for Moss: NO — confirmed NOT safe for aquarium moss (java moss, flame moss,
     willow moss, etc.). Remove moss from tank or isolate before treatment.
   - Safe for Biological Filter: CAUTION — during heavy algae die-off, decomposing algae
     can spike ammonia. Monitor water parameters after treatment.
   - Safe for Scale-less Fish: YES — safe for all fish species
