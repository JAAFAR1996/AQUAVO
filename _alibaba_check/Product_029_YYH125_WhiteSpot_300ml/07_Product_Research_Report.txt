   - Dosage: NOT CONFIRMED from official YEE documentation.
     Typical YEE liquid treatment dosage pattern is 10ml per 100L water, repeated every 2 days
     for 2-3 treatments with 1/3 water change before each dose. VERIFY ON BOTTLE BEFORE LISTING.
   - Treatment Duration: Typically 5-7 days for Ich (3 treatment cycles)
   - Target Disease: White Spot / Ich (Ichthyophthirius multifiliis), fungal infections,
     bacterial infections
   - Safe for Shrimp: UNKNOWN — malachite green-based treatments are generally toxic to
     invertebrates including shrimp. Treat as unsafe until confirmed otherwise.
   - Safe for Plants: UNKNOWN — likely safe at recommended doses but not confirmed
   - Safe for Biological Filter: LIKELY NO — malachite green can harm beneficial bacteria.
     Recommended practice: remove activated carbon, reduce aeration during treatment.
   - Scale-less Fish Warning: Products of this type are typically contraindicated for
     scale-less fish (catfish, loaches, rays). Confirm on label.
