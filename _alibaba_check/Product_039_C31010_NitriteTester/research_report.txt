9. NOTES:
   - PRODUCT CODE PATTERN: C3-1010 prefix shared with Product 038.
       C3-1010-1 = Nitrite (NO2-) test
       C3-1010-3 = Ammonia (NH3/NH4+) test
     These are a companion pair for testing the nitrogen cycle.
   - 100 TESTS vs 60: The nitrite kit yields more tests (~100) than the
     ammonia kit (~60). This is common because nitrite reagents are used
     in smaller quantities per test than ammonia reagents.
   - NITRITE IMPORTANCE: Nitrite is the second danger in the nitrogen cycle.
     After ammonia is converted by Nitrosomonas bacteria, it becomes nitrite
     (NO2-) before Nitrobacter converts it to safe nitrate (NO3-). Testing
     nitrite is essential during the cycling period (first 4-6 weeks) and
     when adding new fish.
   - COMPANION TO BACTERIA: This test kit directly shows whether Products
     036/037 (nitrifying bacteria) are working — if bacteria are active,
     nitrite levels drop.
   - COMPANION TO 038: Products 038 and 039 should ideally be sold together
     as "النيتروجين كيت الكامل" (complete nitrogen cycle test kit).
   - SHARED IMAGE: Same image 043 as Product 038. Source separate photos
