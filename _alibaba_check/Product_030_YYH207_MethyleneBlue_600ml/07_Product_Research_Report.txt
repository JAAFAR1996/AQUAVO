6. SPECIFICATIONS FOUND:
   - Brand: YEE (乐宠) — Weifang Yipin Pet Products Co., Ltd.
   - Product Code: YYH-207
   - Form: Liquid solution (blue-colored)
   - Volume: 600ml
   - Active Ingredient: Methylene Blue (confirmed by product name and retailer descriptions)
   - Dosage: 5ml per 100L of water, once every 2 days, for 2-3 treatment cycles.
     Perform 1/3 water change before each dose. (Confirmed via Betta USA Aquatic listing
     for the YEE Methylene Blue line — applies to YYH series)
   - Treatment Duration: 4-6 days (2-3 treatment cycles, every other day)
   - Target Diseases: White spot (Ich), tail rot, fin rot, saprolegniasis (fungal), gill
     disease, ectoparasites, bacterial infections, body ulcers
   - Additional use: Egg treatment — methylene blue prevents fungal infection of fish eggs
     during hatching
   - Safe for Shrimp: NO — methylene blue is toxic to shrimp and invertebrates
   - Safe for Plants: CAUTION — methylene blue may inhibit photosynthesis; not recommended
     for heavily planted tanks during treatment
   - Safe for Biological Filter: CAUTION — can inhibit beneficial bacteria at higher doses.
     Use lower end of dosage range for established tanks.
   - Safe for Scale-less Fish: Generally considered safer than malachite green but reduce
     dose by 50% for scale-less fish as a precaution.
