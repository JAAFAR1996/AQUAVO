# Carrier statement policy

- Delivery-company fees are recorded separately from other deductions.
- The configured default for Al-Waseet is 5,000 IQD per delivered order.
- Aggregate delivery fees must be exact multiples of the selected company's configured fee.
- A statement difference that is not a delivery-fee multiple must be entered as `other_deduction_amount` with an explanation.
- Example supplied on 2026-08-03: 183,750 gross and 152,750 net imply 31,000 total deductions. Under a 5,000 fee policy, a valid split is 30,000 delivery fees plus 1,000 other deduction, pending confirmation of what the extra 1,000 represents.
- Monthly carrier positions are reconciliation snapshots. Canonical settlement posting remains based on immutable order facts and selected orders.
