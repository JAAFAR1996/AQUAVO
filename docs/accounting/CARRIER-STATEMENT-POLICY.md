# Carrier statement policy

- Delivery-company fees are recorded separately from other deductions.
- The configured default for Al-Waseet is 5,000 IQD per delivered order.
- Aggregate delivery fees must be exact multiples of the selected company's configured fee.
- A statement difference that is not a delivery-fee multiple must be entered as `other_deduction_amount` with an explanation.
