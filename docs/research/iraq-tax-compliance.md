2. **No VAT in Iraq; aquarium equipment is in no enumerated sales-tax category** — MEDIUM-HIGH confidence (B, PwC), but the single most important assumption to confirm. **Do not add VAT/sales tax to product pricing or checkout** until an Iraqi accountant confirms. (T3, U1, U2)

4. **Filing: calendar year, due 31 May; pay within 3 days of assessment; 5-year statute (extendable)** — HIGH confidence (B). ⇒ produce full-year P&L on demand; **retain records ≥5 years** with immutable audit trail. (T4, T5, T6)
