## Highest-impact findings (with confidence)

1. **Income tax: flat 15% of net trading profit** — HIGH (B, PwC). General band, not the 35% oil band. *(T1)*
2. **No VAT; aquarium equipment in no enumerated sales-tax category → do not add VAT/sales tax to pricing or checkout** — MEDIUM-HIGH (B, PwC, re-verified 2026-07-21). The single most important assumption to confirm. *(T3, U1, U2)*
3. **2026 ASYCUDA customs automation verifies import invoices** — MEDIUM (C, news). Every import consignment now needs genuine matching supplier + customs documents; landed-cost paperwork is also customs compliance. *(N1)*
4. **GCT can tax on a deemed/turnover basis if books are weak** — HIGH it's a real risk (B). ⇒ clean, audit-ready, **IUAS-mappable** books are AQUAVO's best tax defense and lower the bill. *(T2, C2)*
5. **Filing: calendar year, due 31 May; pay within 3 days of assessment; 5-year (extendable) statute** — HIGH (B). ⇒ produce full-year P&L on demand; **retain records ≥5 years**, immutable. *(T4, T5, T6)*
6. **Statutory books must map to the Iraqi Unified Accounting System**, signed off by a Syndicate-affiliated auditor — MEDIUM-HIGH (B). *(T7)*
