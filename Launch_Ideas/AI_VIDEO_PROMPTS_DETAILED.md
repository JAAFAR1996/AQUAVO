Serene, captivating, almost magical. The viewer should feel drawn into the

Cinematic, macro photography, National Geographic underwater cinematography,

8K quality, photorealistic, vivid colors, dramatic lighting

MOTION DETAILS:
- Fish Movement Speed: Slow, graceful, unhurried
- Fin Movement: Flowing like silk in slow motion
- Water Movement: Very subtle, almost imperceptible current
- Bubble Movement: Few distant bubbles rising slowly

NEGATIVE PROMPTS:

no aggressive movement, no other fish, no human hands, no aquarium decorations 
in foreground, no flash reflection, no glare on glass
