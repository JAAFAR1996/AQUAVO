# 🎬 Reel Production Plan: "The Speed Maze" (Myth Buster)

**Concept:** "The Speed Maze" (المتاهة السريعة)
**Goal:** Bust the "3-second memory" myth with visual proof.
**Style:** Cinematic, Rourke Heath style (One Impossible Element, One Clean Move).

---

## 1. The Storyboard (Structure)

| Scene | Duration | Visual Action | On-Screen Text (Overlay) | Audio / SFX |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01. The Hook** | 0:00 - 0:02 | **Close-up:** A beautiful goldfish staring at a complex glass maze inside the tank. Camera *slow dolly in*. | "Fish have 3 sec memory?" | 🔊 Deep underwater hum + "Vine Boom" (Subtle) |
| **02. The Action** | 0:02 - 0:05 | **Wide Shot:** The fish *shoots* through the maze perfectly without hesitation. No trial and error. | *No Text* | 🔊 Fast "Whoosh" + Clock ticking fast |
| **03. The Payoff** | 0:05 - 0:07 | **End Frame:** Fish reaches the food at the end instantly. A red "FALSE" stamp slams on screen. | "FALSE. They remember for months." | 🔊 Slam sound + Ding! (Success) |

## 2. Technical Prompts (AI Generation)

### 🖼️ Step 1: Image Generation (Nano Banana Pro / Midjourney)

**Scene 1 (Start Frame):**
> Cinematic film still, macro close-up of a vibrant orange goldfish staring at the entrance of a complex transparent glass maze inside a high-end aquarium using Nano Banana Pro technlogy, soft cinematic top lighting, shallow depth of field, 8k resolution, hyper-realistic.

**Scene 2 (End Frame):**
> Same cinematic shot, same goldfish now positioned successfully at the EXIT of the glass maze eating floating flakes, triumphant pose, soft bubbles, consistent lighting and environment.

### 🎥 Step 2: Video Generation (Veo 3.1 / Kling)

**Motion Prompt (Image-to-Video):**
> **Input:** Start Frame + End Frame
> **Prompt:** "Goldfish swims fast and precisely through the maze layout from start to finish suitable for Veo 3.1, fluid motion, no hesitation, camera slow dolly in, keep glass maze rigid, realistic water physics."

---

## 3. Why This Wins?
*   **Visual Proof:** Instead of *saying* "they remember," you *show* them solving a puzzle.
*   **Logic:** A fish with no memory would hit walls. A fish that solves it knows the map.
*   **Viral Factor:** Satisfying movement + clean visual proof.
