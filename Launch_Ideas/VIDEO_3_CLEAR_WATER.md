### 🥈 الخيار 2: AI (Veo 3 / Kling AI / Runway)

#### المشهد 1: الهوك - المي الصافي (0:00-0:03)

```
Extreme close-up of crystal clear water surface in a glass aquarium. 
Water is calm, slight ripple. Light refraction creates rainbow patterns 
on the surface. Camera pulls back slightly to reveal the water is 
Empty. No fish, no plants. Sterile, lab-like clean feel.

Camera: Macro lens 100mm, f/2.8, sharp focus on water line
Lighting: Cold bright clinical white light (6000K)
Mood: Deceptively clean, sterile, "too perfect"
Audio cue: Unnaturally silent, faint hum of filter
```

---

#### المشهد 2: التوضيح - الانتظار (0:03-0:08)

```
Time-lapse shot of a glass aquarium sitting in a living room corner. 
Day to night lighting transitions. Sun shadows move across the tank. 
The water remains empty of fish. A filter hangs on the back, creating 
gentle surface agitation. Calendar pages overlay effect (add in post).

Camera: Static wide shot, tripod locked
Lens: 24mm wide angle, deep depth of field f/11
Lighting: Natural window light (golden hour to blue hour cycle)
Mood: Marking time, patience, educational
```

---

#### المشهد 3: البكتيريا - الحل (0:08-0:13)

```
Macro shot of white ceramic filter rings (bio-media) inside an 
aquarium filter compartment. The rings are covered in a healthy 
brownish organic biofilm (this is the bacteria). Water flows over 
them gently. High texture detail.

Camera: Macro 100mm, extreme close-up
Lens: Shallow depth of field f/4, focus on texture
Detail: Wet, porous texture of ceramic rings
Color: Earth tones, brown, beige, wet gloss
Style: Scientific documentary, "Life on Earth" macro style
```

---

#### المشهد 4: التحذير - بدونها (0:13-0:17)

```
Close-up of a Goldfish gasping for air near the water surface. 
Water looks slightly milky/cloudy (bacterial bloom). Fish movement 
is sluggish and stressed. Darker, moodier lighting.

Camera: Handheld with slight tension/shake
Lens: 50mm, focus on fish mouth/gills
Lighting: Dim, top-down spotlight, creating shadows
Mood: Warning, urgent, sad
Note: Ensure fish looks "stressed" not "dead" suitable for social media
```

---

#### المشهد 5: CTA - الخاتمة (0:17-0:20)

```
Vibrant, healthy planted tank with a school of Neon Tetras swimming 
in sync against a lush green background of aquatic plants. Bubbles 
rising from plants (pearling). Crystal clear healthy water.

Camera: Slow smooth slider move left to right
Lens: 35mm, crisp details
Lighting: High quality RGB aquarium light, popping colors
Color: Vibrant greens, neon blues and reds
Mood: Success, thriving ecosystem, happy ending
```
