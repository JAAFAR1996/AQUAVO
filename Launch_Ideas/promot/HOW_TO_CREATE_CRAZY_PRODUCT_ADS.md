## Step 1: Capture Your Product

Take a clean, high‑quality photo of your product.

### Optional (Highly Recommended)
Gather inspiration for your ad style using Pinterest or any visual platform of your choice. Focus on:
- Backgrounds
- Camera angles
- Lighting
- Product positioning

---

## Step 2: Generate the Product Ad Image

Open **Nanobanana Pro**, upload your product image, and paste the prompt below. Edit the placeholders based on your product and idea.

### Base Prompt (Image Generation)

```
Create a cinematic, high‑end product advertisement using the uploaded reference image of [INSERT PRODUCT NAME]. Ensure the product remains the main subject, with its exact shape, proportions, textures, branding, and realism preserved from the reference image.

Place the product in a [INSERT ENVIRONMENT / SCENE] (e.g., frozen arctic, tropical beach, urban rooftop, mystical forest). Optionally add atmospheric elements such as [OPTIONAL ATMOSPHERIC ELEMENTS] (e.g., snow, fog, dust, sunlight, mist, reflections) to enhance depth and realism.

Apply dramatic cinematic lighting that matches the scene, such as [INSERT LIGHTING STYLE: soft diffused, harsh directional, rim light, sunset glow, neon], with an overall mood that feels [INSERT MOOD: epic, calm, luxurious, playful, adventurous].

Use a [INSERT CAMERA ANGLE: low‑angle hero shot, top‑down, close‑up macro, wide cinematic shot] to emphasize the product’s [INSERT ATTRIBUTE: strength, elegance, performance, luxury].

Apply [INSERT COLOR PALETTE: warm, cool, teal‑blue cinematic, vintage, high‑contrast] color grading. Add subtle, realistic environmental effects on the product such as [INSERT EFFECTS: frost, dust, reflections, water droplets, wear and tear], while maintaining photorealistic textures and reflections.

The final output should be ultra‑high resolution, studio‑grade, professional product advertising quality in a [INSERT BRAND QUALITY: luxury brand, global brand, award‑winning photography style]. Avoid cartoonish or overly stylized effects.

Include any additional brand emotion or vibe in [OPTIONAL NOTES].
```

### Alternative Method (If You Used Inspiration Images)

If you followed the optional inspiration step, do this instead:

1.  Go to **ChatGPT**
2.  Upload:

3.  **Prompt for ChatGPT:**

```
The first attached image is my raw product image.

Write a detailed, professional prompt to create a high‑end product advertisement where everything blends together naturally and realistically.
```

ChatGPT will generate a refined prompt that you can directly paste into Nanobanana Pro to get a strong product ad image.

## Step 3: Animate the Product Ad

Once you have your final product image:

1.  Take your **raw product photo**
2.  Take the **generated product ad image**
3.  Use **Seedream 1.5 Pro** with the **Start‑to‑End Frame** feature
4.  Paste the prompt below and customize it.

### Base Animation Prompt

```
A single hand firmly grips [PRODUCT NAME], fingers wrapped naturally around it. The grip subtly tightens as the wrist makes a quick micro‑adjustment — a realistic, instinctive movement showing control and intent.

Without hesitation, the arm snaps upward and throws the product forcefully into the air like a solid physical object. The fingers fully open as the product cleanly leaves the hand.

The [PRODUCT NAME] travels upward with believable weight and momentum, rotating slightly from the force of the throw. Its ascent slows naturally as gravity takes over.

The background — [ENVIRONMENT / LOCATION] — blurs subtly beneath it, reinforcing depth and motion.

At the exact apex of the throw, a brief bullet‑time freeze activates.

The product hangs suspended mid‑air as the camera smoothly orbits around it, revealing:
- Material texture
- Surface details
- Edges, contours, and form
- Reflections and light interaction

Lighting is [LIGHTING TYPE: natural, studio, soft daylight].

No visual effects. No stylization. No exaggeration.
Only raw materials, shape, and realism.

Bullet‑time ends instantly. Gravity resumes.

The product drops straight down and impacts [GROUND / SURFACE TYPE], compressing it naturally on contact. Subtle secondary motion occurs in the surrounding environment — dust, foliage, fabric, debris, or surface texture reacting realistically.

The product settles naturally into place.

Final frame locks into a strong hero shot of [PRODUCT NAME] resting on [SURFACE].
Shallow depth of field.
Crisp detail.
Balanced composition.
Grounded physical presence.

No slow motion.
No cinematic tricks.
No stylized effects.
Pure physical realism.
High‑end product ad feel.
```

### Example (Action Camera)

```

Without pause, the arm snaps upward and the camera is thrown forcefully into the air like a solid object, leaving the hand cleanly as the fingers fully open.

No effects. No embellishment. Just form and material.

The final frame locks into a bold hero shot of the action camera resting in moss. Shallow depth of field. Soft forest light. Crisp detail.
Pure physical realism.
```
