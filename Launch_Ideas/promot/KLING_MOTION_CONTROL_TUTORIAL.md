# How to Create AI Videos with Kling Motion Control & Kling O1

To create this video, you’ll need two tools: **Kling Motion Control** and **Kling O1**.

## Part 1: Changing Clothes, Background, and Face

### Cloth Change & Face Swap

For cloth changes, background and face swaps, we’ll use **Kling Motion Control**.

I personally use it on **Higgsfield**, as it has a cleaner UI and gives access to all essential AI tools under one subscription.
👉 **Access it here:** [https://goto.higgsfield.ai/3JNnKr](https://goto.higgsfield.ai/3JNnKr)

**Process:**

1.  **Record a video** of yourself showing the first frame you want to use. Take a **screenshot** of that frame.
2.  **Upload the screenshot** to **NanoBanan Pro** with a prompt like this (edit for your needs):

    ```
    Replace the person’s outfit perfectly with [dress/character name], without changing anything else. Do not resize, move, or alter anything else.
    ```

3.  Take the generated result and head to **Motion Control → Video Tab** on Higgsfield.
4.  Upload your **original video** and the **image generated** by NanoBanan Pro.
5.  Use this prompt:

    ```
    Transfer the motion from the attached video to the attached image perfectly.
    ```

6.  Hit **Run**, and voilà! Your outfit is changed in any video.

### Background Change

1.  Choose your desired background from Pinterest or any creator.
2.  Ask **ChatGPT** to analyze the background and create a detailed **NanoBanan Pro** prompt. For example:

    ```
    Create a prompt to place my photo into this background perfectly, matching lighting and perspective.
    ```

3.  Paste the ChatGPT-generated prompt in **NanoBanan Pro** with your face image and hit **Generate**. You’ll get an image of yourself on the new background.
4.  Back in **Kling Motion Control**, upload **any video** you want to change the background of, along with the **NanoBanan-generated background image**.
5.  Hit **Run**—it will replace the background in your video.

### Face Swap

Follow the same steps as above. Use a **NanoBanan Pro** prompt like:

```
Replace the person perfectly with [character name] in his signature outfit, without changing anything else. Do not resize, move, or alter anything else.
```

*Edit the prompts as needed for your own project.*

## Part 2: Adding VFX to Hands

1.  Go to **NanoBanan Pro** and ask it to add any **light VFX** to your image. Avoid heavy effects for better results.
2.  Take your **original video** and the **VFX image**, then upload both to **Kling O1**.
3.  Use a simple prompt like:

    ```
    Add VFX like in [example photo], matching light and motion perfectly.
    ```

4.  Hit **Run**, and your video will have the VFX applied seamlessly.
