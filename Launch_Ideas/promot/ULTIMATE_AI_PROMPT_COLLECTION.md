## 📊 Slide 3 – Turn Complex Balance Sheets Into Simple Infographics

**Turn this balance sheet into a clean, Apple-style minimal infographic.**

**Layout:** Two columns
*   **Left:** Things the company owns
*   **Right:** Things the company owes + what’s left

**Assets (Things the company owns):**
*   **Cash & Bank** → Money they have (coins/bills icon)
*   **Stuff they use to make money** → Big stuff they own (buildings/machines icon)
*   **Other stuff** → Other things (box icon)
*   Show relative size using stacked bars or icons

**Liabilities (Things the company owes):**
*   **Short-term** → Bills to pay soon (small red boxes / minus icons)
*   **Long-term** → Bills to pay later (larger red boxes)
*   Show risk with a color bar: green = safe, yellow = caution, red = risky

**Equity (What’s left after paying bills):**
*   **What’s left** → blue pie or stacked chart
*   Show percentage of total assets

**Extra Investor Hints:**
*   **Cash vs debt** → small bar or gauge (green = safe, red = risky)
*   **Growth** → up/down arrow for trend
*   **Overall health** → traffic-light-style indicator

**Style:** Pastel colors, flat icons, thin lines, rounded corners, minimal, clean, and easy to read.
