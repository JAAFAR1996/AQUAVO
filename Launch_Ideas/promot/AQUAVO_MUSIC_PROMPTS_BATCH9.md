## 🧠 قواعد الموسيقى الإدمانية — 2026

| القاعدة | التفصيل |
|---------|---------|
| **Sound-Synced Transitions** | كل انتقال لقطة = مربوط بـ beat drop أو عنصر موسيقي |
| **Tempo = Mood** | بطيء (70-85 BPM) = تشويق/حزن · متوسط (90-110) = فضول · سريع (115+) = حماس |
| **Key Shift = Emotional Arc** | التحول من Minor → Major عند الحل يخلق ارتياح عصبي |
| **Sound Design Layers** | الموسيقى + مؤثرات صوتية (whoosh/splash/click) = غمر سمعي كامل |
| **Loop-Friendly** | الثانية الأخيرة تتصل بالأولى بسلاسة → يعيد المشاهد التشغيل |

## ① R48 — حوض مهمل → تحفة فنية ⏱️

### البروموت:
```
Dramatic transformation reveal instrumental. 95 BPM. Opens with a single dripping water sound into a hollow space — echoey, neglected. A lo-fi distorted piano plays two depressing descending notes in Eb minor — broken, out of tune, like a neglected instrument. Gritty texture: tape hiss, vinyl crackles, low rumble. The 'disgusting' section. Then a SNAP — a clean fingersnap at exactly beat 1 of bar 5. The key shifts UP to G major — bright, clean, pristine. A smooth bass guitar groove enters, followed by crisp brushed drums. Marimba plays a rising pattern — each note higher than the last, like building something beautiful. Layers add progressively: warm pad, light shaker, gentle bells — each layer representing another step of the restoration. By the final reveal: FULL celebratory arrangement — acoustic guitar strumming, triumphant horns (not overpowering), sparkly glockenspiel arpeggios. The music literally transforms from ugly to beautiful alongside the video. Think: home renovation show reveal moment. No vocals. Repulsive, then deeply satisfying.
```
