Here’s the full workflow I used to bring this idea to life 👇

---

### 1️⃣ Concept & Base Image – Midjourney

I start in **Midjourney** to explore ideas and lock the overall vibe.

This is where I experiment with:

- Different compositions
- Lighting setups
- Atmosphere and mood

I iterate until I get a **strong base image** that feels like the right “world” for the scene.

This image becomes the visual foundation for everything that comes next.

---

### 2️⃣ Multi-Angle Generation – Nano Banana Pro

Once the base image works, I move to **Nano Banana Pro**

(inside **Freepik** or **Higgsfield**, depending on the project).

Here, I ask it to generate **multiple angles of the same scene**:

- Same character
- Same environment
- Different camera positions and framings

The goal is to make it feel like a **real shoot**, not just one lucky frame.

This is how I build a full “shot list” of consistent angles from a single concept.

---

### 3️⃣ Camera Motion – Kling 2.5 (First-to-Last Frame)

Then I go into **Kling 2.5** and use the **First to Last Frame** feature.

This allows me to create **smooth transitions** between two still images,

almost like a digital **Bolt Cam** move:

- The camera “flies” from one angle to the next
- The shot feels continuous, not cut
- It adds a strong cinematic feeling to the sequence

---

### 4️⃣ Edit & Timing – DaVinci Resolve

Once I have all my clips, I edit everything in **DaVinci Resolve**.

Here I:

- Cut and reorder shots
- Adjust timing and pacing
- Speed up or slow down specific moments

The goal is to keep the **motion intense and dynamic**,

so the reel feels tight, intentional, and cinematic from start to finish.

---

### 5️⃣ Sound Design – Making It Feel Real

Last part: **sound design**.

This is where everything really comes to life.

I add:

- Impacts and hits
- Whooshes and transitions
- Ambience and subtle details

These sounds make the motion feel **physical**, not just visual.

It’s a big reason why the final result feels like a **real ad**,

and not just a series of AI tests.

---

That’s the full pipeline:

from **one idea → multi-angle shoot → motion → edit → final cinematic reel**.
