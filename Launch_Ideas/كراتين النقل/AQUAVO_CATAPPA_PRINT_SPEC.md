## 4. SURFACE FINISHES — OUTER SLEEVE

The outer sleeve requires **4 finishing techniques** applied in this order:

| # | Finish | Coverage Area | Details |
|---|--------|--------------|---------|
| 1 | **CMYK Offset Print** | Full sleeve surface | Deep Forest Green background (PMS/CMYK equivalent of #1B3A2D) + Ivory text (#F5F0E8) |
| 2 | **Soft-Touch Matte Lamination** | Full sleeve (all sides) | Velvety matte finish — fingerprint resistant, premium tactile feel |
| 3 | **Gold Hot Foil Stamping** | "AQUAVO" wordmark (front) + leaf icon + thin border accents | Warm Gold metallic foil (equivalent to #C8A96E) |
| 4 | **Spot UV Gloss Varnish** | "PREMIUM CATAPPA EXTRACT" text + leaf illustration (front only) | High-gloss transparent coating on specific areas — creates matte vs. gloss contrast |
| 5 | **Blind Debossing** | Background leaf pattern on front panel | No ink — pressed into surface only. Terminalia catappa leaf shapes. Subtle — visible at angles. |

### Finish Layering Order:
```
Print (CMYK) → Lamination (Soft-Touch Matte) → Hot Foil → Spot UV → Debossing
```
