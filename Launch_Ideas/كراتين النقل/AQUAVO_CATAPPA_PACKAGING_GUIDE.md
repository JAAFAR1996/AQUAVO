## 1. 🔥 2026 Luxury Packaging Trends (Applied to This Product)

### 1.1 Box Structure — **Drawer-Style (Sliding Box)**

The **drawer box** (also called a sliding box) is THE luxury packaging format for 2026. The outer sleeve slides off to reveal an inner tray holding the pyramid bags — creating a ritualistic unboxing experience.

| Element | Specification |
|---------|--------------|
| **Structure** | Two-piece drawer box (outer sleeve + inner tray) |
| **Opening** | Horizontal slide-out drawer |
| **Closure** | Friction-fit (no magnets needed — the soft-touch finish creates natural grip) |
| **Inner Tray** | Rigid 1200gsm greyboard with paper-wrapped finish |
| **Divider** | Optional: a subtle tissue paper layer on top of bags for the "reveal" moment |

### 1.2 Materials — **Eco-Luxury**

| Component | Material | Why |
|-----------|----------|-----|
| **Outer Sleeve** | 350gsm FSC-certified art board | Premium rigidity + eco-certification |
| **Inner Tray** | 1200gsm greyboard (recycled core) wrapped in 157gsm art paper | Structural strength with luxury surface |
| **Tea Bags** | PLA biodegradable pyramid bags (corn-based) | 100% compostable, food-safe |
| **Tea Bag String** | Unbleached cotton thread | Natural aesthetic, plastic-free |
| **Tea Bag Tag** | 300gsm FSC kraft or textured cotton paper | Tactile, eco-friendly |
| **Inks** | Soy-based inks throughout | Non-toxic, low environmental impact |

### 1.3 Surface Finishes — **Multi-Sensory Contrast**

This is where 2026 luxury packaging truly shines — the combination of **three finishes** on a single surface:

| Finish | Application Area | Effect |
|--------|-----------------|--------|
| **Soft-Touch Matte Lamination** | Entire outer sleeve | Velvety, fingerprint-resistant texture. Consumers instinctively slow down and touch it. |
| **Spot UV (Gloss Varnish)** | Logo mark, leaf illustration, "Premium" text | Creates a dramatic matte-vs-gloss contrast. The glossy elements "pop" and catch light. |
| **Gold Hot Foil Stamping** | "AQUAVO" wordmark, border accents, small leaf icon | Lustrous metallic shimmer. Signals premium quality instantly. Rose Gold is also an option. |

### 1.4 Color Palette

| Color | Hex Code | Usage |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Deep Forest Green** | `#1B3A2D` | Primary background (outer sleeve) — evokes nature + aquatic depth |
| **Warm Gold** | `#C8A96E` | Foil stamping, accent lines, typography highlights |
| **Ivory / Off-White** | `#F5F0E8` | Secondary text, inner tray surface, tag background |
| **Matte Black** | `#1A1A1A` | Regulatory text, barcode area |

### 1.5 Additional Trend Elements

- **🌿 Debossed Leaf Pattern** — Subtle, blind-debossed (no ink) pattern of Terminalia catappa leaves across the sleeve. Visible only at certain angles and when touched. Adds a hidden tactile layer.
- **📱 QR Code Integration** — Small, elegantly framed QR code on the back linking to AQUAVO's brewing guide video or website.
- **♻️ Sustainability Badge** — Printed (not stickered) certification icons: FSC, PLA Compostable, Cruelty-Free.
- **🎭 Tonal Holography** (optional upgrade) — Iridescent color-shifting ink on the leaf illustration for a "living" surface effect.
