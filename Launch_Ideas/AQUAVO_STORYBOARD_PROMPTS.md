# 🎬 AQUAVO Storyboard Prompts
## "The 3-Second Myth" - Visual Storyboard Guide

Use these prompts with your preferred AI Image Generator (Midjourney, Ideogram, etc.) to create a professional storyboard.

---

## 🎨 Global Style Settings
**Style:** Cinematic commercial, high-end product photography, warm golden hour lighting, hyper-realistic, 8k resolution.
**Aspect Ratio:** `--ar 16:9` (for video frames)

---

## 📸 Panel 1: THE HOOK (The Myth)
**Concept:** A giant stopwatch shattering over a city.
**Goal:** Shock viewer, visual metaphor for breaking time myths.

**Prompt:**
```
A hyper-realistic cinematic wide shot of a giant vintage brass stopwatch drifting in the sky above a modern city skyline at sunset. The stopwatch glass is shattering explosively into shards. In the foreground on a rooftop, a small glass fishbowl with a bright orange goldfish inside. The fish looks shocked. Golden hour lighting, dramatic atmosphere, high contrast, 4k, --ar 16:9
```

---

## 📸 Panel 2: THE TRUTH (The Reality)
**Concept:** The shards turn into a golden number "5".
**Goal:** Show the "5 months" truth in a surreal, beautiful way.

**Prompt:**
```
Surreal cinematic shot. Floating jagged glass shards in the sky are melting and transforming into liquid gold. The liquid gold forms a massive, glossy number "5" floating in the air. Inside the golden number "5", a goldfish wears a tiny black graduation cap. Warm magical lighting, sparkles, surreal art style, dreamlike, --ar 16:9
```

---

## 📸 Panel 3: THE BOND (Emotion)
**Concept:** POV from inside the fishbowl looking at the owner.
**Goal:** Show connection and recognition.

**Prompt:**
```
POV shot from inside a round glass fishbowl looking out at a human face. The human face is smiling warmly and close to the glass. A digital HUD overlay on the glass shows green text "OWNER RECOGNIZED" and a heart icon. Bubbles in the water. Intimate, emotional, sci-fi interface element, warm colors, --ar 16:9
```

---

## 📸 Panel 4: THE DREAM (Care)
**Concept:** Living room transformed into an underwater paradise.
**Goal:** Show how the fish *should* feel (Aquavo standard).

**Prompt:**
```
Surreal interior design shot. A modern living room completely filled with clear blue water. A giant, car-sized goldfish swims gracefully in the center of the room. A human sits calmly on a floating sofa reading a book. Sunlight streams through the water from above. Peaceful, serene, magical realism, teal and orange color palette, --ar 16:9
```

---

## 📸 Panel 5: THE CTA (Engagement)
**Concept:** Fish asking "What's your fish?"
**Goal:** Drive comments and sales.

**Prompt:**
```
Extreme close-up of a cute goldfish face. The fish is winking playfully with one eye. In the foreground, a speech bubble says "WHAT'S YOUR FISH?". The background is a blurred teal underwater scene. High detailed texture on scales, pixar-style charm but realistic textures, bright and inviting, --ar 16:9
```

---

## 📝 Instructions for Storyboard Assembly
1.  **Generate Images:** Run the prompts above in Midjourney (`/imagine`) or your tool of choice.
2.  **Select Best:** Choose the best image for each panel.
3.  **Arrange:** Place them in order (1-5) on a canvas (Canva, Photoshop).
4.  **Add Text:** Add the script/captions under each image:
    *   **Panel 1:** "They say fish have a 3-second memory..."
    *   **Panel 2:** "Wrong! It's actually 5 MONTHS."
    *   **Panel 3:** "They can even recognize their owners!"
    *   **Panel 4:** "Your fish deserves a smart home."
    *   **Panel 5:** "Comment your fish type for 20% OFF!"

This storyboard will serve as the **perfect blueprint** for your video editor or for manual assembly using stock footage/simple animation.
