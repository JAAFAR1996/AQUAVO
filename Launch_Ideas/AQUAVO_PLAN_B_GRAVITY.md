# 🛸 Plan B: "The Gravity Shift" (The Safe Route)
**Goal:** A viral visual that **Veo 3.1** can handle perfectly.
**Why this will work:** Veo struggles with *fast speed* + *glass refraction* (causing "melting"). This concept uses **Slow Motion** + **Air** (Clean background), which plays to Veo's strengths.

---

## 🍌 Phase 1: The Images (Nano Banana)
*We need two images of the fish "swimming" in the air.*

### 🖼️ Shot 1: The Escape (Start Frame)
**Concept:** The fish has just jumped out and is looking at the camera, floating.

```text
Cinematic film still, medium shot of a vibrant orange Goldfish FLOATING IN MID-AIR inside a cozy modern living room.

ACTION:
The fish is not flopping; it is SWIMMING in the air as if it were water. Its fins are fanned out gracefully.

DETAILS:
Behind it is a blurred fish bowl on a table (showing where it came from). Water droplets are frozen in the air around it like diamonds.
Lighting is soft afternoon "Golden Hour" sun coming from a window side.

TECHNICAL:
8k, photorealistic, shallow depth of field (bokeh background), 50mm lens, magical realism style.
```

### 🖼️ Shot 2: The Journey (End Frame)
**Concept:** The fish is swimming further away, comfortable in its new "air ocean".

```text
Cinematic film still, medium shot of the SAME orange Goldfish swimming deeper into the living room air.

ACTION:
It is now near a green houseplant leaf, inspecting it. It looks calm and majestic.

CONSISTENCY:
Same fish, same living room background, same golden hour lighting.

TECHNICAL:
8k, photorealistic, 50mm lens.
```

---

## ⚡ Phase 2: The Movement (Veo 3.1)
*This prompt focuses on "Weightless Fluidity".*

**Upload:** Start + End Frames.
**Prompt:**
```text
A cinematic slow-motion shot of a goldfish swimming through the air.

PHYSICS:
The fish moves with "Zero Gravity" fluid physics. It does not fall. It swims slowly and gracefully through the air.
The fins undulate softly like silk.
The frozen water droplets around it drift very slowly in 3D space (parallax).

CAMERA:
Slow, subtle handheld movement following the fish. 24fps.

ATMOSPHERE:
Dreamy, magical, dust motes dancing in the sunlight beams.
```
