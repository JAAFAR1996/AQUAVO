## 🎥 The Director's Vision (Technical Specs)

### 1. The Look (Visuals)
*   **Camera Lense:** **100mm Macro Lens**. We want a tight, compressed background.
*   **Depth of Field:** **f/2.8**. The fish is razor sharp, the maze walls behind it blur slightly into a "creamy bokeh". This screams "High Budget".
*   **Lighting Strategy (Crucial):**
    *   **Key Light:** Soft Top-Down (mimicking high-end aquarium lights) to create **Caustics** (water shimmy patterns) on the fish's scales.
    *   **Backlight:** A subtle "Cool Blue" rim light from behind to separate the fish from the dark background.
    *   **Fill:** Negative fill (black cards) on sides to keep contrast high. No flat lighting!

### 2. The Sound (Audio Architecture)
*Do not just put music. Build a soundscape.*
*   **Layer 1 (Base):** Deep, low-frequency "Underwater Rumble" (ASMR White Noise).
*   **Layer 2 (Tension):** A faint, fast "Ticking Clock" or "Heartbeat" that speeds up as the fish moves.
*   **Layer 3 (Action):** "Water Woosh" sounds (subtle) when the fish turns corners.
*   **Layer 4 (The Reveal):** Silence... then a sharp "Ding" or "Stamp Sound" when "FALSE" appears.
*   **Music:** Dark, rhythmic, minimal. Think "Oppenheimer" tension, not "Happy Aquarium".
