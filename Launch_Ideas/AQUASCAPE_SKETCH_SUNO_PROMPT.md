## 1️⃣ برومبت تعديل الصورة (إضافة النص + اللوقو)

> استخدم هذا البرومبت بـ ChatGPT / أي أداة AI لتحرير الصور. ارفقلها الصورتين: رسمة الأكواسكيب + لوقو أكوافو.

```
I'm providing two images:
1. A hand-drawn pencil aquascape layout sketch on white paper (the main image)
2. The Aquavo brand logo (fish infinity symbol with "AQUAVO" text)

TASK: Extend the canvas and add branded text + logo while keeping the original sketch 100% untouched.

─── STEP 1: EXTEND THE CANVAS ───
• Expand the image canvas by adding generous white margins:
  - TOP: add ~25% extra white space above the sketch
  - BOTTOM: add ~15% extra white space below the sketch
  - LEFT & RIGHT: add ~5% extra white space on each side
• The new margins must seamlessly match the existing paper color/texture (off-white, slightly warm tone)
• The original sketch must remain perfectly centered within the new expanded canvas

─── STEP 2: ADD ARABIC TEXT (TOP-RIGHT AREA) ───
• In the newly created top margin, place the following RIGHT-TO-LEFT Arabic text, aligned to the RIGHT side:

  Line 1: مخطط أولي لسكيب حوض 100x50 سم
  Line 2: رؤية أكوافو لتفاصيل الخشب والنباتات.
  Line 3 (add slight vertical spacing before this line): رأيكم؟

• Font: Clean, modern sans-serif (similar to Cairo, Tajawal, or IBM Plex Arabic)
• Color: Dark charcoal grey (#3A3A3A) — NOT pure black
• Size: Line 1 & 2 should be medium weight (regular/medium), Line 3 "رأيكم؟" should be slightly BOLDER and slightly larger to draw attention
• Text must be crisp, sharp, and anti-aliased — NOT blurry or pixelated
• Text must sit entirely ABOVE the sketch border — zero overlap with the drawing

─── STEP 3: PLACE AQUAVO LOGO (BOTTOM-RIGHT AREA) ───
• Place the provided Aquavo logo in the bottom-right corner of the new margin area
• Size: Small — approximately 10-12% of the total image width
• The logo should be subtle and professional, not overpowering
• Apply a slight opacity reduction (~85-90% opacity) so it feels like a watermark/brand stamp rather than a sticker
• The logo must sit entirely BELOW and to the RIGHT of the sketch border — zero overlap with the drawing
• Maintain the original logo colors (teal/dark navy)

─── CRITICAL CONSTRAINTS ───
• DO NOT alter, redraw, crop, filter, color-shift, sharpen, or modify ANY pixel of the original pencil sketch
• DO NOT add any shadows, borders, frames, or decorative elements
• DO NOT change the sketch's line quality, contrast, or paper texture
• The dimension labels (100cm, 50cm, ~45cm, ~55cm) must remain fully visible and unobstructed
• The small substrate diagrams in the bottom-left and bottom-right corners of the sketch must remain fully visible
• The final image should look like a professional architect/designer sharing their hand-drawn concept with simple, elegant branding
• Overall feel: minimal, clean, professional — as if printed on a premium matte presentation board
```

### 🏆 الخيار الأول: Ambient Aquascape (الأفضل — هادي + مائي)

**Style/Tags:**
```
ambient, aquatic soundscape, ethereal, tranquil, no vocals, instrumental
```

**Prompt:**
```
Ambient aquascape soundscape with warm evolving synth pads, soft felt piano chords, gentle water flowing and bubbling sounds, and subtle shimmering bell tones. Ethereal, tranquil, and deeply calming. Minimal rhythm, no drums, no vocals, seamless atmospheric flow. The feeling of sunlight dappling through clear aquarium water onto green plants and smooth river stones.
```

**Lyrics (اتركه فارغ أو اكتب هيج):**
```
[Instrumental]
[Intro] [Warm ambient pads, gentle water effects, distant bell shimmer]
[Main] [Soft piano melody enters, slow evolving harmonies, aquatic bubbling]
[Outro] [Fade out with flowing water sounds and sustained pad]
```

## 3️⃣ أفكار عرض الرسمة بالريلز 🎬

| الطريقة | الوصف | مناسب لـ |
|---------|-------|----------|
| **Time-Lapse Sketching** | سجّل فيديو سريع لعملية الرسم من الصفر للنهاية | أعلى تفاعل |
| **Before → After** | ابدأ بالرسمة الفارغة وبعدين اكشف التصميم النهائي بـ transition | ريلز قصير 15 ثانية |
| **Annotated Pop-ups** | اعرض الرسمة مع نصوص تطلع animated تشرح كل عنصر (خشب، نباتات، حجر) | تعليمي |
| **Split Screen** | نص الشاشة: الرسمة — النص الثاني: الحوض الحقيقي أو ref image | مقارنة |
| **رأيكم؟ Poll** | اعرض الرسمة + ستكر تصويت "حلوة / تحتاج تعديل" | تفاعل عالي |
