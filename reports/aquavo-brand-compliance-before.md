## Baseline drift

- 310 source matches remain for legacy colors/fonts including `#199BB8`, coral, amber, Outfit or Poppins.
- `client/index.html` declares the legacy primary and coral accent.
- Active header/footer use the old public logo and gradient styling.
- Favicons, manifest, OG defaults, JSON-LD and email templates are not yet aligned to one approved v2 asset set.
- The active experience mixes multiple navigation styles, glow effects, gradients and continuous motion that weaken premium consistency.
- Customer copy includes unsupported or overly strong statements such as blanket “100% original,” direct-import claims, products tested personally and a founder story without supplied evidence.

## Compliance target

The website must express a calm Iraqi premium equipment brand: dark authority surfaces, clean proof areas, precise cyan flow accents, short Baghdadi copy, evidence-first trust and no invented specifications, prices, reviews, urgency or authority claims.
